📖 Materi Quantifiers

Kata yang menunjukkan jumlah makanan & minuman

Apa itu Quantifier?

Quantifier adalah kata yang digunakan untuk menunjukkan jumlah atau ukuran sesuatu. Dalam bahasa Inggris, kita sering menggunakannya untuk makanan dan minuman.

1. Quantifying Nouns (Satuan Ukuran)

Kita menggunakan pola: a / an + satuan + of + makanan/minuman

🍦
a cone of ice cream
sebuah kerucut es krim
🥛
a glass of milk
segelas susu
🍞
a slice of bread
sepotong roti
🍫
a bar of chocolate
sebatang cokelat
🍌
a hand of bananas
sisir pisang
🥣
a bowl of soup
mangkuk sup
🥤
a bottle of water
sebotol air

sepotong roti (utuh)

sepiring nasi

sekaleng soda

secangkir teh
⚖️
a kilo of sugar
satu kilogram gula

2. Countable vs Uncountable

Countable (bisa dihitung): apple, banana, cookie, egg → pakai many, a few, some, any, a lot of

Uncountable (tidak bisa dihitung): rice, water, milk, bread, sugar → pakai much, a little, some, any, a lot of

Quantifier Countable Uncountable Contoh
many ✅ ❌ many apples
much ❌ ✅ much water
a few ✅ ❌ a few cookies
a little ❌ ✅ a little milk
some ✅ ✅ some rice / some bananas
any ✅ ✅ any juice? / any apples?
a lot of ✅ ✅ a lot of food

3. Contoh Kalimat

  • I want a cone of ice cream. (Saya ingin sebuah kerucut es krim.)
  • She needs a glass of water. (Dia butuh segelas air.)
  • There are many bananas on the table.
  • There isn't much milk left.
  • Can I have some rice, please?
  • Do you have any juice?
  • We bought a lot of fruit.
  • There are only a few cookies left.
  • Add a little sugar to the tea.
💡 Tips: Gunakan some di kalimat positif, any di kalimat negatif & pertanyaan. Much/many lebih sering di pertanyaan & negatif, sedangkan a lot of cocok untuk kalimat positif.
